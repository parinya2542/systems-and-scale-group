Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Who we areInformation we collectHow we use itYour rightsContact

1. Who we are

Systems and Scale Group Ltd is a UK technology consultancy. For the purposes of UK data protection law, we are the controller of personal information collected through this website and our direct business communications.

You can contact us at privacy@systemsandscale.co.uk or by post to our business correspondence address in London, United Kingdom.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • contact details you provide, such as your name, work email address, telephone number and organisation;
  • information included in an enquiry, proposal request or other correspondence;
  • technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information and basic website usage data;
  • cookie preferences and, where you consent, aggregated analytics information.

We do not intentionally collect special category data through this website. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary and we have agreed a secure method.

3. How we use personal information

4. Sharing and international transfers

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, communications, security, analytics and professional advice. They may process information only for agreed purposes and under appropriate contractual safeguards. Where data is transferred outside the United Kingdom, we use recognised safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement/addendum where required.

5. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements. General enquiries are normally reviewed for deletion after 24 months unless they lead to an ongoing client or supplier relationship.

6. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information from accidental loss, unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. No internet transmission is completely secure, so please use appropriate care when sending information online.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies

Our use of cookies is described in the cookie policy. You can change optional cookie choices at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email privacy@systemsandscale.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity before completing a rights request.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our practices or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on a device when you visit a website. They can support essential functions, remember preferences or help website owners understand how a site is used.

2. Cookies used on this website

The base website does not activate optional analytics until a visitor provides consent. If analytics tooling is added later, this table and the consent implementation should be updated before deployment.

3. Necessary cookies

Necessary cookies or local storage support core website functions, including remembering your cookie choice. They cannot be switched off through our preference panel because the panel would otherwise be shown repeatedly.

4. Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand broad patterns such as which pages are visited and whether visitors encounter usability problems. They should be used only in aggregated form and only after consent.

5. Managing your choices

You can open cookie settings at any time. You can also remove cookies or local storage through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect some site features.

6. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, delete or block cookies. The exact steps vary by browser and device. Use your browser’s help or privacy settings for current instructions.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@systemsandscale.co.uk.
